Models

Lightweight carbon-fibre construction and turbocharged power are the common thread of McLaren's road cars. Here are six of the range.

Picture: McLaren W1

McLaren W1

Hypercar

The flagship, unveiled in October 2024 and limited to 399 cars. It is the spiritual successor to the F1, P1 and Senna.

  • Engine: 4.0 L twin-turbo V8 hybrid
  • Power: about 1,258 PS
  • 0-100 km/h: 2.7 s
  • Top speed: 350 km/h
View details
Picture: McLaren 750S

McLaren 750S

Supercar

The lighter, sharper successor to the 720S, built for both road and track.

  • Engine: 4.0 L twin-turbo V8
  • Power: 750 PS (about 740 hp)
  • 0-100 km/h: 2.8 s
  • Top speed: 332 km/h
View details
Picture: McLaren 750S Spider

McLaren 750S Spider

Supercar

The same performance as the coupe, with a retractable hard top for open-air driving.

  • Engine: 4.0 L twin-turbo V8
  • Power: 750 PS (about 740 hp)
  • 0-100 km/h: 2.8 s
  • Top speed: 332 km/h
View details
Picture: McLaren Artura

McLaren Artura

Supercar

McLaren's first series-production hybrid supercar, with a new V6 engine and an electric motor.

  • Engine: 3.0 L twin-turbo V6 plug-in hybrid
  • Power: about 680 PS
  • 0-100 km/h: 3.0 s
  • Top speed: 330 km/h
View details
Picture: McLaren Artura Spider

McLaren Artura Spider

Supercar

The open-top version of the Artura, keeping the hybrid powertrain and carbon-fibre structure.

  • Engine: 3.0 L twin-turbo V6 plug-in hybrid
  • Power: about 680 PS
  • 0-100 km/h: 3.0 s
  • Top speed: 330 km/h
View details

McLaren GTS

GT

The grand tourer of the range: more luggage space and comfort for long journeys, with McLaren performance.

  • Engine: 4.0 L twin-turbo V8
  • Power: 635 PS (about 626 hp)
  • 0-100 km/h: 3.2 s
