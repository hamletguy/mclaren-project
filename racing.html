Racing

Racing is where McLaren started and where it still proves itself. The Formula 1 team is one of the most successful in the sport's history.

Picture: the McLaren F1 team

The team

McLaren Racing is based at the McLaren Technology Centre in Woking, England. The team has competed in Formula 1 since 1966 and has won the Constructors' Championship in 1974, 1984, 1985, 1988, 1989, 1990, 1991, 1998, 2024 and 2025. It is led by CEO Zak Brown and team principal Andrea Stella.

Drivers

Picture: Lando Norris

Lando Norris

Nationality: British

Born in Bristol in 1999. He made his F1 debut with McLaren in 2019 and became World Champion in 2025.

Picture: Oscar Piastri

Oscar Piastri

Nationality: Australian

Born in Melbourne in 2001. He joined McLaren in 2023 and won his first Grand Prix in Hungary in 2024.
